# Music studio

Make music from a few words, then shape it into a song on tracks. Music
studio runs the music models of your own computer, so your songs are files
you keep: no credits, no download limits, no watermark.

## What you can do

- **Make a piece from words.** Describe it, tap a few style words, set its
  length, and press **Make**. The music model of your computer makes it.
- **Choose between versions.** Ask for up to four versions of each piece in
  the app's settings, listen to each, and keep the one you like.
- **Bring your own sound.** Import a song or a recording, or drop it on the
  studio. Each file gets a track of its own.
- **Play it and save it.** Play the song with every track at once, and
  export it as a WAV file.

## What it needs

- A **music model** on Hearthscale Server. If your computer has none, the
  studio lists the ones your Server can install.
- The models differ: some sing, some make only instrumental music, and they
  take different lengths. The studio offers only what the chosen model can do.

## What it asks for

- **Its own data**: your songs, kept in the app's database on your computer.
- **Model jobs**: music made by the music model you choose.
- **Models**: the list of music models, and the install of one when you ask.
