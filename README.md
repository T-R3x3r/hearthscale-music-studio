# Music studio

Make music from a few words, then shape it into a song on tracks. Music
studio runs the music models of your own computer, so your songs are files
you keep: no credits, no download limits, no watermark.

## What you can do

- **Make a piece from words.** Describe it, tap a few style words, set its
  length, and press **Make**. With a model that sings, write lyrics by
  section, or let the chat model write them for you.
- **Change what you made.** Give a clip a new style, make some of its bars
  again, make it longer, or add a part such as drums. The clip keeps its
  own sound as its first version, so nothing is lost.
- **Choose between versions.** Ask for up to four versions of each piece in
  the app's settings, listen to each, and keep the one you like.
- **Shape it on tracks.** Move clips, loop them by their corner, trim and
  cut them, fade them, mute or solo a track, set its volume and pan, and
  give it a warmer, brighter or roomier sound. Undo and redo every step,
  and save named versions of the song.
- **Bring your own sound.** Import a song or a recording, drop it on the
  studio, or open a sound file with Music studio. Record your voice with a
  count-in and a metronome. Split any clip into its parts: vocals, drums,
  bass and the rest.
- **Keep snippets.** Save any clip as a snippet and drop it into another
  song. A snippet of another tempo or key is fitted to the song.
- **Ask in your own words.** The assistant beside the timeline makes,
  changes, splits and mixes for you, and each change is one step of undo.
- **Save it.** Export the song as a WAV file, or each track into a folder.

## What it needs

- A **music model** on Hearthscale Server. The studio lists the ones your
  Server can install. Models differ: some sing, some make only
  instrumental music, and only some change existing audio. The studio
  offers only what the chosen model can do.
- A **separation model**, such as HTDemucs, to split clips into parts.
- A **chat model** for lyrics and the assistant.

## What it asks for

- **Its own data**: your songs, versions and snippets, in the app's
  database on your computer.
- **Model jobs**: music and splits made by the models you choose.
- **Models**: the list of models, and the install of one when you ask.
- **The microphone**: only while you record.
- **Folders you choose**: only when you export each track into a folder.
- **Conversations**: the assistant's chats.
